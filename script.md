# Miami Art Week Opportunities – Conversational Script

*For the ArtVision Podcast Special Segment*
*Voice: Encouraging, uplifting friend over coffee. Casual but authoritative. Never punching down.*

---

Hey, grab a seat — this coffee’s on me. Miami Art Week is right around the corner (November 30 through December 6, 2026, with Art Basel public days December 4-6 at the Convention Center). The big fairs have mostly closed their calls, but there are still a couple of solid doors open for artists, especially if you’re working digitally or in that Web3-adjacent space. I went digging so you don’t have to. Here’s what’s still accepting, straight and clear.

First up, and this one is free, no fee at all: Loop Art Critique Cohort 24 with guest Ana María Caballero, in collaboration with the Institute of Contemporary Art, Miami. They’re looking for media artists — digital, interactive, video, immersive, or even physical work that can live as digital representations. It’s anonymous selection based purely on the art. You submit exactly five pieces (images, videos, links, or docs of sculpture/install — nothing with your name on it). No bio, no statement, no CV required. Deadline is January 4, 2027. Selected artists get six weeks of critique sessions (Mondays and Thursdays, 12-3 PM Eastern, January 20 to March 1), a $100 honorarium, six months free access to XR Studio for building in the spatial internet, and then a collectively curated exhibition that lives online in the Spatial Internet *and* is shown on a screen at ICA Miami. Exhibition opens March 19, 2027. It’s not physically on the beach during the week itself, but the ICA Miami tie-in and the digital/Web3 vibe make it a strong one for anyone in that space. Apply here: https://form.jotform.com/ariel_ariel777/anamariacaballero or just Google “Loop Art Critique Ana María Caballero open call”. Questions on the form are minimal — basically contact info plus the five anonymous works. Super low barrier.

Next, if you’re local and want something physical right during the week: Backyard Basel at the Coral Gables Museum on Friday, December 4, 6-10 PM. It’s a free public outdoor art market during Gallery Night, with music, food, community energy. Local artists 18+ can apply for a table to show and sell work ($50–$5,000 range). There is a $50 fee, but it goes toward keeping the museum’s free cultural events running — solid community justification, and the event itself is free for everyone else. You upload three image samples (JPG/PNG). Deadline is rolling while tables last. Google “Backyard Basel Coral Gables Museum 2026” or go straight to https://coralgablesmuseum.org/backyard-basel-2026-artist-application/. It’s not Web3-specific, but if you have physical work and you’re in South Florida, it’s a real chance to be out there while the city is buzzing.

A lot of the bigger digital projection and official fair calls (Render Network’s Miami After Dark, OneLoveArtDAO at Hotel Yve, arteNXT, etc.) already closed in October or mid-November. Those were strong while they lasted. For pure free digital with institutional Miami ties and almost zero paperwork, Loop is the one I’d jump on right now. Check the database for more entries as we add them daily — search “Miami” plus the keyword and the URL will pop.

You’ve got this. Go make something beautiful and get it in front of people. I’ll keep hunting.
